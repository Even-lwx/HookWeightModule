CubeMX GPIO settings for ADS1232

PB0: GPIO_Input, No pull
PB1: GPIO_Output, Push-Pull, No pull, Low speed, default level Low
PB2: GPIO_Output, Push-Pull, No pull, Low speed, default level Low

USART1: PA9 = TX, PA10 = RX, asynchronous, 115200-8-N-1.

ADS1232 configuration pins are not controlled by the three MCU wires:
for AIN1, tie A0=0 and TEMP=0; for gain 64 tie GAIN1=1 and GAIN0=0;
for 10 SPS tie SPEED=0. Do not leave these digital inputs floating.

In main.c USER CODE sections:

  #include "ads1232.h"
  #include "ads1232_service.h"

After MX_GPIO_Init() and MX_USART1_UART_Init():

  (void)ADS1232_Init();

Inside while (1):

  ADS1232_Service10Hz(&huart1);
